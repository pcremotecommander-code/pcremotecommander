Privacy & data controls

What PCRC processes, why it is needed, and how to close your account.

Effective November 8, 2026. Operated by Janagiri, India, under the product name PC Remote Commander. Privacy contact: janagiri3297@gmail.com.

How the connection works

Your AI client sends requests to the PCRC gateway. The gateway authenticates the account, checks permissions, and routes allowed requests to the selected computer. The local agent returns results through the gateway to your AI client. HTTPS protects transport. This is not end-to-end encryption that hides requests from the gateway.

Requested files, terminal output, browser content and screen captures may contain personal data and can enter your AI conversation. Choose your workspace and permissions carefully. Your AI provider has its own retention and privacy terms.

Beta.9 interactive agent terminals display bounded request and result previews locally. Recognized secret fields, authentication values and images are omitted, but previews can still contain private task content. PCRC does not create a separate trace file for these previews; your terminal or operating system may retain scrollback. Consider this before sharing your screen or terminal output.

Background cleanup and recovery copies

Maintenance runs every five minutes and processes five accounts per run, rotating through the account directory. Physical cleanup can occur after a validity period ends; failures are retried on later sweeps. Large accounts may need multiple cleanup passes. We do not claim a guaranteed deletion completion time during beta.

Cloudflare SQLite Durable Object point-in-time recovery can retain earlier database states for up to 30 days. Active-service deletion does not instantly erase provider recovery history. Recovery must preserve closed-account restrictions before access resumes. The production restore drill is still pending.

Close your account

Open Account & privacy, review the consequences, and type DELETE. All subscriptions must already be fully canceled. PCRC blocks computer access, disconnects device connections, revokes assistant grants, removes account data and clears the public account directory. Cleanup can continue in the background if a provider is unavailable. Commands already accepted by a computer cannot necessarily be stopped.

A minimal account-ID closure marker and deletion time remain to enforce closure. Closure is permanent in the self-service flow. Contact support before attempting to rejoin. This does not delete your GitHub account, AI conversations, files on your computer, or independently retained Paddle records. Supabase Auth identity removal in this beta follows the verified manual request process described above.

Providers, access and your requests

Paddle sandbox checkout is for test payments only; live checkout charges real money. The billing page identifies the mode. When you view localized pricing, your browser contacts Paddle, which uses your IP address to estimate your country, currency and applicable tax. PCRC displays Paddle’s current formatted quote without retaining that preview or adding a fixed exchange rate.

GitHub provides optional social sign-in; Supabase provides email/password sign-in when enabled; the configured SMTP service delivers account emails; Cloudflare hosts the gateway and storage; npm distributes the launcher; Paddle processes billing; your chosen AI provider processes its conversation. Their infrastructure may process data outside India. The owner console provides support, account status, usage, subscription management and public prompt publishing; it does not grant the operator control of users' computers.

We use account and connection metadata to provide the requested service, protect access, enforce quotas and handle support. For access, correction, deletion assistance or a privacy concern, email the contact above from an address you control. We may ask you to verify account ownership through sign-in. Do not email passwords, access tokens, pairing codes, private keys or identity documents. Changes to this notice will be dated on this page.
