# An Apology to the PCRC Community

*An important update from the PCRC developer.*

Dear PCRC Users,

First, I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience and service interruptions you may have experienced with PC Remote Commander (PCRC).

I want to be transparent about what is happening.

PCRC was running on Cloudflare's free infrastructure plan at the time of the interruption. We reached the daily usage limit for Cloudflare Durable Objects, which are an important part of how PCRC manages connections and communication between your AI assistant and your computer.

When this limit is reached, Cloudflare can restrict further usage until the quota resets. This can result in connection failures, interruptions, or temporary unavailability of some PCRC features.

### Why did this happen?

PCRC is currently developed and maintained by one person. I'm handling development, infrastructure, testing, bug fixes, security, and user support myself.

I'm doing my best to keep PCRC accessible without charging users, but free infrastructure has limitations, especially as usage grows.

This is not an excuse for the inconvenience. I take responsibility for improving the reliability of PCRC.

### What am I doing about it?

I'm investigating the underlying connection and resource-consumption issues, working on infrastructure improvements, and testing changes carefully before applying them to the production service.

My goal is to make PCRC more stable and reliable without compromising user security or existing functionality.

### How can you support PCRC?

If you find PCRC useful and believe in what we're building, your support would mean a lot.

You can help by:

- Sharing PCRC with other developers and AI enthusiasts.
- Reporting bugs and sharing feedback.
- Supporting the project financially, if you're able to.
- Giving the project a star on GitHub and helping more people discover it.

Your support can help us move toward more reliable infrastructure, better testing, and faster improvements.

I'm building PCRC with a long-term vision: making powerful computer control accessible through AI assistants, without making personal users pay just to get started.

Thank you for your patience, understanding, and for being part of this journey.

I'm sorry for the inconvenience, and I genuinely appreciate everyone who continues to believe in PCRC.

**— The PCRC Developer**
